# LUPUS-CORE

**Autonomous Behavioral Intelligence Framework**

*Instinct is inherited. Intelligence is earned.*

LUPUS-CORE is an experimental behavioral intelligence framework designed to bring autonomy, individuality, and emergent behavior to virtual creatures.

Originally envisioned for **Family Time**, the project aims to transform traditional scripted NPCs into autonomous agents capable of making context-aware decisions, remembering experiences, developing distinct personalities, and forming meaningful social relationships.

Rather than relying on predefined behavioral sequences alone, LUPUS-CORE explores interconnected systems of instinct, cognition, memory, emotion, and social intelligence to create believable, evolving behavior.

### Core Objectives

- **Autonomy:** Enable agents to independently evaluate their surroundings and make decisions.
- **Instinct:** Model natural behaviors, survival priorities, and environmental responses.
- **Memory:** Retain experiences that influence future decisions.
- **Personality:** Support persistent individual traits and behavioral development.
- **Emotion:** Introduce internal states that dynamically influence behavior.
- **Social Intelligence:** Model attachment, trust, cooperation, and relationships between agents.

### Project Vision

**Not merely to simulate intelligent behavior, but to create the impression of a living mind behind every character.**

LUPUS-CORE is currently in its conceptual and experimental stages. Its long-term goal is to provide a modular, extensible behavioral architecture that can be adapted to other simulation environments.
